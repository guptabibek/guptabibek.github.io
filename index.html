ForecastPro

Forecasting and AI reporting for retail pharmacy chains

Read the case study →
Context
Multi-tenant SaaS in production with a few large retail pharmacy chains in India. It syncs their ERP data, forecasts demand and answers questions asked in plain English.
My role
Designed and built it end to end, as the only engineer.
Delivered
  • Natural-language reporting: an LLM turns a question into a validated semantic query, which is compiled to secure, parameterized SQL. The model never writes SQL.
  • AI insights: 12 providers flag stock-out risk, churn, dead stock and discount anomalies.
  • Prepaid AI credits on an append-only ledger, with idempotent Stripe webhooks.
