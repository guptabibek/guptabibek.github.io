ForecastPro

Multi-tenant forecasting SaaS with natural-language reporting

Demand forecasting and supply planning for distributors, fed by a resumable ERP sync pipeline, with a custom NLQ engine that answers business questions in plain English.

  • Custom NLQ engine: an LLM maps each question to a catalog-constrained semantic query, compiled to parameterized SQL with tenant and branch row security. The model never writes SQL.
  • AI insights: 12 pluggable providers flag stock-out risk, churn, dead stock, and discount anomalies, and compute a business health score.
  • AI credit billing: append-only ledger enforced by database triggers, reservation-based charging, and idempotent Stripe webhooks.
